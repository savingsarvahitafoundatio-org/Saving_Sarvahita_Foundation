Compassion on the Streets: Street Animal Feeding & Hunger Support Drive Across Mumbai Suburbs

Saving Sarvahita Team
•
August 2026
•
Mumbai Suburbs & Dahisar

Every living being deserves empathy, nourishment, and care. Driven by our core commitment to Samarpan (Compassion & Transformation), the Saving Sarvahita Foundation team organized a dedicated Street Animal Hunger Support & Feeding Drive across several street pockets and suburban localities in Mumbai.

Protecting Mumbai's Voiceless Strays

Stray dogs and street animals in urban environments struggle every single day to find clean drinking water and nutritious food. Scavenging from garbage dumps leads to severe digestive illnesses, malnutrition, and dehydration. Our volunteer squad stepped onto the ground to serve fresh, protein-rich dog meals, wholesome biscuits, and pure milk bowls in safe roadside corners.

Key Highlights of the Feeding Drive:

Over 100+ Street Animals Fed
Nutritious Dog Meal Bowls
Fresh Milk & Hydration Stations
High-Energy Biscuits Distributed
Gentle Interaction & Health Check
Encouraged Community Empathy

Spreading Kindness One Meal at a Time

Beyond feeding, our volunteers spent time observing the general health of the street dogs, checking for physical injuries or skin infections. By placing food and water in clean, biodegradable paper bowls away from active traffic zones, we ensured peaceful, stress-free feeding for the animals without disrupting neighborhood residents.

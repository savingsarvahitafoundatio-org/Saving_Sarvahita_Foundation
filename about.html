OUR STORY & PURPOSE

Dedicated to Compassionate Social Upliftment

Saving Sarvahita Foundation is a newly established Section 8 non-profit organization registered under the Government of India. Our foundation is built on the core values of Seva (Service), Sahayog (Collaboration), and Samarpan (Transformation).

We are focused on bringing grassroots change in child education, emergency healthcare, street animal care, senior citizen protection, women empowerment, hunger relief, and environmental sustainability.

Our Vision

To heal and uplift underprivileged lives while operating as a 100% transparent non-profit platform.

Our Mission

Expand non-profit healthcare, educational, and welfare services across Maharashtra and India.

BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Meet Our Leadership

The dedicated leaders guiding Saving Sarvahita Foundation towards meaningful social transformation.

Aakash Mantappa Katti

Director

"I never imagined that one day I would start an NGO. Like many people, I was busy building my own career and life. But over the years, I kept meeting families who needed help and children who had dreams but no support to achieve them. Every such experience stayed with me longer than I expected."

"One day, I realised that waiting for someone else to make a difference wasn't the answer. If I had the ability to help, even in a small way, I should start. That thought became the foundation of Saving Sarvahita Foundation."

"My goal is simple—to build an organisation that people can trust. Whether we help a child continue their education, support an elderly person, or stand beside a family during difficult times, I want every effort to be genuine, transparent, and made with respect for human dignity."

Vedant Sanjeevkumar Pal

Director

"I've always believed that helping people doesn't require a special occasion. Sometimes all it takes is showing up when someone has nowhere else to turn. While working with different people and listening to their stories, I understood that many problems can be solved when someone genuinely cares enough to take the first step."

"When Aakash shared the idea of creating Saving Sarvahita Foundation, I knew I wanted to be part of that journey. Today, my focus is to ensure that every initiative is carried out responsibly and reaches the people it is meant for."

"For me, this Foundation is about earning trust through actions, not words. If our work can bring hope to even one family, we know we're moving in the right direction."

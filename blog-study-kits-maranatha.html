COMPLETED DRIVE REPORT

Empowering 22 Young Minds: Free Study Kits Distribution Drive at Maranatha Harvest Mission Orphanage

Saving Sarvahita Team
•
August 2, 2026
•
Chembur West, Mumbai

Education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty. With this firm belief, the Saving Sarvahita Foundation successfully conducted a comprehensive Study Kits & School Essentials Distribution Drive for 22 wonderful children residing at the Maranatha Harvest Mission Orphanage Society in Chembur West, Mumbai.

Bringing Smiles & Bright Futures

Many orphanages face challenges in providing fresh learning supplies for every child at the beginning of each academic session. Our team visited the orphanage to personally interact with the children, understand their educational goals, and distribute customized study kits tailored for their school needs.

What Was Included in Each Kit:

  • High-quality School Bags
  • Notebooks & Writing Pads
  • Geometry Boxes & Instruments
  • Pens, Pencils & Erasers
  • Drawing Books & Color Sets
  • Water Bottles & Stationery Pouch
